# Accessibility Statement

We want Bright Numbers to be usable by every child, including children with learning disabilities, attention differences, autism, motor challenges, low vision and emerging reading skills.

## Built-in supports

- **VoiceOver:** every button, answer choice and picture has a spoken label.
- **Dynamic Type:** text scales with the size chosen in iOS Settings → Accessibility, plus an in-app "larger text" option.
- **Read aloud:** questions, hints and feedback can be spoken by the device, at an adjustable speed.
- **Large touch targets:** main action buttons are at least 44 points tall and most are 64 or larger.
- **No time limits by default:** regular practice is never timed. The optional Speed Round is off unless a grown-up turns it on.
- **Reduced motion:** animations can be turned off in the App; the setting starts out matching the system Reduce Motion setting.
- **High contrast:** an always-light, high-contrast mode is available.
- **Sensory controls:** vibration can be turned off.
- **Error tolerance:** wrong answers are never punished; after two tries the answer is shown and the child moves on.
- **Concrete visuals:** dot pictures accompany problems so children can count instead of relying on reading.

## Known limitations

- The App is currently available in English only.
- Switch Control and Full Keyboard Access work through standard iOS support but have not been separately tested on every screen.

## Feedback

If you or your child have difficulty using any part of the App, please tell us at **dca.support@icloud.com**. Accessibility reports are treated as bugs and prioritized. This statement describes our goals and current state; it is not a warranty or guarantee of conformance to any accessibility standard.
