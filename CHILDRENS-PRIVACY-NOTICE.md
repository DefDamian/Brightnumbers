# Children's Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026

This notice is for parents and guardians. Bright Numbers is made for elementary school children, including children who may receive special education supports.

## What we collect from children

**Nothing.** The App does not collect, use, or disclose personal information from children, as that term is defined in the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506) and the FTC's COPPA Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 312), or in the GDPR, UK GDPR, or any similar law.

Specifically, the App does not collect:

- first or last names (the optional nickname typed by a grown-up stays on the device only)
- home, email or other addresses, phone numbers, or contact information
- screen names, user names, or account identifiers
- photos, videos, voice or audio recordings
- precise or approximate geolocation
- persistent identifiers such as device IDs, cookies, IP addresses, or advertising identifiers
- any information about disabilities, diagnoses, education plans, grades, or school

## No parental consent is required

Because no personal information is collected, verifiable parental consent under COPPA is not required. We still place a "grown-ups only" gate in front of Settings, Progress and these legal documents so that children do not change settings or reach contact details by accident, in line with Apple's Kids Category guidelines.

## No advertising, purchases, or outside links

The App contains no advertising of any kind, no in-app purchases, no social features, no chat, no user-generated content, and no links out of the App in any area a child can reach.

## Progress data

Practice results (stars, questions answered, accuracy) are stored only on the device and shown only to whoever holds the device. They are not linked to any identity, and we never see them.

## Your rights as a parent

Since we hold no data about your child, there is nothing for us to review, correct, or delete, and nothing to refuse further collection of. You can remove all locally stored data at any time by deleting the App. If you believe the App has collected information from a child in any way, contact us immediately at **dca.support@icloud.com**; we will investigate and respond promptly.
