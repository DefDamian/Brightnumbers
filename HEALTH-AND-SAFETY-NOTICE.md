# Health & Safety Notice

Please read before allowing a child to use Bright Numbers.

## Adult supervision

The App is designed for young children. A responsible adult should set up the App, choose settings appropriate for the child, and supervise use, including the length of screen time.

## Screen time and breaks

Follow the screen-time guidance of your pediatrician and local health authorities. Encourage regular breaks, good posture, and viewing from a comfortable distance. Stop use if a child reports eye strain, headache, or discomfort.

## Motion, flashing and sensory sensitivity

The App uses mild animations, confetti effects, color, optional sound effects, and optional vibration. It does not intentionally include rapid flashing, but any screen content may affect sensitive individuals. If a child has a history of photosensitive seizures, motion sensitivity, or sensory processing differences, enable "Reduce motion and animation" and turn off "Vibration feedback" in Settings before use, and discontinue use immediately if the child shows any adverse reaction, such as dizziness, nausea, disorientation, eye twitching, involuntary movements, or loss of awareness. Consult a doctor before resuming.

## Sound and hearing

The read-aloud feature and sound effects play audio and may play even when the device's silent switch is on. Sound effects can be turned off in Settings. Set the device volume to a comfortable level before use, especially with headphones, to avoid hearing damage.

## Device safety

Keep the device, charger and cables away from small children when not in use. Do not allow children to use the device while walking or in any situation that requires attention.

## Timed mode

The optional Speed Round is a timed game and is off by default. Some children find time pressure stressful. A grown-up decides whether to turn it on, and should turn it off if a child becomes upset or anxious.

## Rewards are not real

Stars, stickers, packs and chests are in-app collectibles only. They have no monetary value, cannot be bought, sold, traded or transferred, and are not a promise of any real-world reward.

## Not a medical device

The App is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Nothing in the App is medical advice.
