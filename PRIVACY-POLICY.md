# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026
**App:** Bright Numbers
**Data controller / publisher:** Damian Cedeno
**Contact:** dca.support@icloud.com

## 1. Summary

Bright Numbers does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information from anyone, adult or child. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no third-party software. The App works fully offline. The only thing that can leave your device is your child's game progress, and only to **your own iCloud account** through Apple's iCloud service, if you leave "Sync progress with iCloud" turned on. We cannot see, access or retrieve that data.

## 2. Information stored on your device only

The App saves a small amount of information in its own private storage **on the device it is installed on**:

- **Settings** you choose (read-aloud on or off, speech speed, text size, contrast, haptics, motion, number of questions, difficulty, answer mode).
- **An optional first name or nickname** that a grown-up may type in Settings so the App can say hello. It is optional, may be anything, can be erased at any time, and is never transmitted.
- **Practice progress** (stars, questions answered, accuracy per topic, daily practice streak, the date of last practice, speed-round high scores).
- **Game rewards** (which stickers have been collected, unopened sticker packs, today's quest progress, streak milestones reached). Stickers are built-in emoji; no images are downloaded.
- **A record that the Terms were accepted** (version number and date).
- **The parent code** (4–6 digits chosen by the grown-up), stored as a salted hash in the iOS keychain on this device only. It is never synced to iCloud or transmitted. If the grown-up unlocks with Face ID or Touch ID, that check is performed entirely by iOS; the App never receives or stores any biometric data.

This information is stored using standard iOS app storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include the App's data as part of the normal device backup under Apple's own privacy terms, which we do not control.

## 3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, request or access:

- names (other than the optional on-device nickname above), email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, dates of birth, or government identifiers;
- photos, videos, audio, or files;
- contacts, calendars, reminders, or health data;
- precise or coarse location;
- device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), IP addresses, or any persistent identifier;
- information about disabilities, diagnoses, IEP or 504 status, grades, school, or any sensitive category of data;
- usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics beyond what Apple itself collects under your iOS settings and Apple's privacy policy.

The App does not use the microphone, Bluetooth, local network, push notifications, or background activity. The camera and photo library are used only as described in Section 3B. It makes no network requests of its own; the only network activity is Apple's iCloud service syncing the progress data described in Section 3A, when that setting is on.

## 3A. iCloud sync (optional)

If "Sync progress with iCloud" is on (the default) and the device is signed into iCloud, the App stores a copy of the **progress and rewards data listed in Section 2** (not the nickname, not settings) in the iCloud Key-Value storage belonging to the signed-in Apple Account, so that stars, stickers, quests and streaks follow the child between the family's iPhones and iPads. This data:

- is transmitted only between your device and Apple's iCloud servers, encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple;
- is stored under your Apple Account and governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy;
- contains no name, no identifier and no personal information: only game counts such as "stars: 14" and "stickers: panda ×2";
- is never sent to us, and we have no technical means to read it.

Turn it off at any time in Grown-ups → "Sync progress with iCloud". Resetting progress in the App also clears the iCloud copy; a second device that still has progress will upload it again unless you reset there too, since the reset is applied device by device. If the device is not signed into iCloud, the App behaves exactly as if the setting were off.

## 3B. Homework scanning (camera and photos)

A grown-up can scan a printed worksheet (behind the grown-up gate) so the child can practice those problems in the App. When this feature is used:

- the camera, or a photo you choose from your library, is used **only while you are on the scanning screen**;
- the image is read by Apple's on-device text recognition to find math problems such as "7 + 5";
- the image is **processed in memory on the device and then discarded**. It is not saved by the App, not added to your photo library, and never uploaded anywhere;
- only the problems found (for example "7 + 5") are kept, as part of the practice round, and only on the device;
- we never see the image or the problems.

The App asks for camera permission the first time you tap "Scan with camera". You can deny it and use "Choose photo" instead, or type problems in by hand. The "Show me how" explanations are generated by fixed rules inside the App, not by any online service or artificial intelligence.

The read-aloud feature uses the speech synthesizer built into iOS and iPadOS. Speech is generated on the device. Nothing is recorded, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## 8. Data retention and security

Data stored by the App remains on your device until you delete it (Section 9) or delete the App. A copy of progress data may remain in your iCloud account until you reset progress in the App or remove the App's data in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. We retain nothing because we receive nothing. The App's local data is protected by the device's built-in encryption and passcode protections provided by iOS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your device itself.

## 9. Deleting data

- **Erase progress:** Progress screen (behind the grown-up gate) → "Reset all progress".
- **Erase settings and the nickname:** Settings (behind the grown-up gate) → "Reset settings to defaults", then clear the name field.
- **Erase everything:** delete the App. iOS removes all of its data with it.
- **Erase the iCloud copy:** tap "Reset all progress" while iCloud sync is on, or turn sync off and delete the App's iCloud data from iPhone Settings.
