# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026
**App:** Bright Numbers
**Data controller / publisher:** Damian Cedeno
**Contact:** dca.support@icloud.com

## 1. Summary

Bright Numbers does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information from anyone, adult or child. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no third-party software and no internet connection required. Everything the App does happens on your device and stays there.

## 2. Information stored on your device only

The App saves a small amount of information in its own private storage **on the device it is installed on**:

- **Settings** you choose (read-aloud on or off, speech speed, text size, contrast, haptics, motion, number of questions, difficulty, answer mode).
- **An optional first name or nickname** that a grown-up may type in Settings so the App can say hello. It is optional, may be anything, can be erased at any time, and is never transmitted.
- **Practice progress** (stars, questions answered, accuracy per topic, daily practice streak, the date of last practice, speed-round high scores).
- **Game rewards** (which stickers have been collected, unopened sticker packs, today's quest progress, streak milestones reached). Stickers are built-in emoji; no images are downloaded.
- **A record that the Terms were accepted** (version number and date).

This information is stored using standard iOS app storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include the App's data as part of the normal device backup under Apple's own privacy terms, which we do not control.

## 3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, request or access:

- names (other than the optional on-device nickname above), email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, dates of birth, or government identifiers;
- photos, videos, audio, or files;
- contacts, calendars, reminders, or health data;
- precise or coarse location;
- device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), IP addresses, or any persistent identifier;
- information about disabilities, diagnoses, IEP or 504 status, grades, school, or any sensitive category of data;
- usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics beyond what Apple itself collects under your iOS settings and Apple's privacy policy.

The App does not use the camera, microphone, Bluetooth, local network, push notifications, or background activity. It makes no network requests.

## 4. Text to speech

The read-aloud feature uses the speech synthesizer built into iOS and iPadOS. Speech is generated on the device. Nothing is recorded, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## 5. Children's privacy

The App is directed to children. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR and UK GDPR), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), and similar laws by the simplest means available: we do not collect personal information from anyone. Because we collect no personal information, no parental consent is required under COPPA. See the separate **Children's Privacy Notice**.

## 6. Legal basis and international users (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Because the App does not collect or process personal data on our systems, we do not act as a data controller or processor of your personal data in the sense of the GDPR. The only processing that occurs is on your own device, under your control, and is not transferred to any country. Should any processing by us ever be introduced, we will update this Policy, identify the legal basis, and where required obtain consent before it begins.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, to opt out of sale or sharing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights (including under the CCPA/CPRA for California residents, and under the GDPR for residents of the EEA, UK and Switzerland). Because we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, delete or port. You exercise full control directly on your device (Section 9). If you nevertheless wish to make a rights request or believe we hold information about you, email **dca.support@icloud.com** and we will respond within the time required by applicable law (30 days for GDPR, 45 days for CCPA). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**California "Do Not Sell or Share":** We do not sell or share personal information and never have. **Notice at collection:** we collect no categories of personal information.

## 8. Data retention and security

Data stored by the App remains on your device until you delete it (Section 9) or delete the App. We retain nothing because we receive nothing. The App's local data is protected by the device's built-in encryption and passcode protections provided by iOS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your device itself.

## 9. Deleting data

- **Erase progress:** Progress screen (behind the grown-up gate) → "Reset all progress".
- **Erase settings and the nickname:** Settings (behind the grown-up gate) → "Reset settings to defaults", then clear the name field.
- **Erase everything:** delete the App. iOS removes all of its data with it.

## 10. Third parties and links

We do not share data with any third party because we have none to share. The App contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics providers, social media plug-ins, or links to external websites in areas accessible to children. The Apple App Store itself, and Apple's operating system, are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

If we ever change how the App handles information, we will update this Policy, change the effective date, present it for acceptance in the App, and describe the change in release notes. We will never begin collecting personal information without first disclosing it here and obtaining any consent required by law.
